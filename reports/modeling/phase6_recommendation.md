# Phase 6 recommendation — not started

Phase 5 freezes one train-only uncalibrated logistic pipeline and an illustrative top-10%
encounter outreach policy. Any following work should package that exact artifact for an
explicitly labeled portfolio demonstration, with a versioned input/output contract and
reproducible loading of the original bytes. Do not quietly retrain on train+validation or
replace the policy because of final test results.

Before a demonstration, agree on missing/invalid field handling, discharge eligibility,
confirmed-destination timing, batch definition, deterministic ties and how repeat patients
are shown. A 10% rule needs an available batch; a single-patient score cannot establish a
capacity cutoff. Explain that neither estimated risk nor a SHAP contribution proves causation.

Direct clinical use requires separate work: contemporary external/temporal and prospective
validation; field-arrival and utilization-coverage audits; clinical review of rehabilitation
and low-utilization failure patterns; actual staffing/repeat-contact rules; evaluation of
contact burden, subgroup outcomes and intervention effectiveness. The historical dataset
cannot supply these missing requirements.

No FastAPI service, Streamlit dashboard, Docker image, production serving architecture,
deployment, final resume bullets or recruiter polish was created in Phase 5. Those tasks
require the user's next phase instruction.
